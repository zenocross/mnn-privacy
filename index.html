Privacy Policy

Marites News Network (MNN) · Facebook application “Media Platform Manager”
Effective 1 October 2026 · Last updated 1 October 2026

Marites News Network (“MNN”, “we”) operates a Facebook Page that publishes commentary on Philippine news. This policy explains what data the MNN application handles, why, and how to contact us about it. It covers the Facebook application Media Platform Manager and the MNN Facebook Page.

What this application does

The application is an internal publishing tool. It reads publicly available news articles, prepares post drafts for human review, and publishes approved posts to the MNN Facebook Page. Every post is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published. The application is not offered to the public, has no sign-in, and no one other than the Page operator uses it.

Information we collect

  • Our own Facebook Page credentials. An access token for the MNN Page, which we hold in order to publish to that Page. The Page is one we own and administer.
  • Our own published content and its metrics. Posts we publish, and the engagement figures Facebook reports for them.
  • Publicly available news material. Article text, publication names, dates, and images gathered from public news websites for research and attribution.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, request, store, buy or sell personal information about visitors to our Page or about any other Facebook user. We do not access your profile, friends, messages, photos or contact details. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers or analytics cookies, and this policy page itself sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

Third parties

To research and prepare posts, the application sends article URLs and article text to third-party web search, content-extraction and AI language-model providers. The material sent is public news content and our own editorial drafts. We do not send personal information about Page visitors to these providers, because we do not hold any. We do not share, sell or rent any data to advertisers or data brokers.

Storage and retention

Research material, drafts and publication records are stored on infrastructure we control, and are retained as an editorial record for as long as the Page operates. Access credentials are kept in server configuration and are not shared. We delete material on request where it is ours to delete, as described below.

Your rights, and how to make a request

If you believe we hold information relating to you, or you want material concerning you removed from our Page or our records, write to us and we will respond. Where a request concerns content we published, we will correct or remove it if it is inaccurate or if we have no proper basis to publish it. Philippine residents have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including rights of access, correction and erasure.

Data deletion instructions

To request deletion of any data associated with you, or of content published about you, email ritwic@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request, including enough detail to identify the material (for example a link to the post). We will confirm receipt and complete or refuse the request, with reasons, within thirty days. To remove the application's own access to our Facebook Page, the Page administrator can revoke it in Facebook Page settings under Business Integrations.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date above.
